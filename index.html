FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about Loqelo and our upcoming launch.

What is Loqelo?

Loqelo is a planned marketplace connecting customers with service providers for home, garden and office needs. Customers will be able to describe a job, receive offers and choose a provider.

When will Loqelo launch?

Loqelo is planning to launch in February 2027. An exact launch date has not yet been announced. Join the waitlist for updates.

Is Loqelo available worldwide?

Loqelo is collecting pre-launch interest from multiple countries. The countries where services will be available at launch have not yet been confirmed.

How do I become a service provider?

Choose “Join as a Provider,” enter your email address and service country, and consent to launch updates. This joins the pre-launch interest list; platform registration will come later.

What services will I find?

Loqelo is being developed around home, garden and office needs, with additional service categories previewed in the platform designs. Final launch coverage may vary.

What details does the waitlist require?

An email address and country are required. You can optionally indicate whether you are signing up as an individual or a business. Consent to project updates is separate and required to subscribe.
