Cookie Policy

The website uses an essential HTTP-only session cookie named LOQELO_SID to help protect waitlist submissions and administrator access. Optional analytics preferences are stored in this browser so you can change your choice.

Google Analytics 4 is disabled by default and its script is requested only after you select “Accept analytics” and the site owner configures a valid measurement ID. You can use “Cookie settings” on the homepage to review or change your preference.

Owner checklist: confirm host infrastructure cookies, any CDN or security cookies, consent documentation, retention periods and any future analytics or support integrations.
