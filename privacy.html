Privacy Policy

The Loqelo pre-launch website requests your email address, country, account role, and optional account type when you join its waitlist. Your consent to receive launch updates is recorded with a timestamp. This data is held in the site owner's private contact list for managing the pre-launch programme.

Authorized administrators can review, export, mark contacts as unsubscribed, or delete records. The site uses essential session cookies to protect form submissions and admin access. Google Analytics 4 only loads when you opt in to optional analytics, and no email address, form text or other contact fields are sent to it.

Consent is voluntary. Before launch, Loqelo must publish the identity and contact details of its data controller, purposes and lawful bases, data-retention schedule, processors, storage locations and applicable rights and transfer disclosures. For data access or deletion, please contact the official Loqelo privacy contact once the company provides one.

Owner checklist: add official legal entity; postal address; privacy email; legal jurisdiction; retention window; subprocessor details; rights and complaint contact; confirm how launch emails and unsubscribe links will be handled.
